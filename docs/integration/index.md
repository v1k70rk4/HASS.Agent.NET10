# The Home Assistant integration

The Home Assistant side is a custom integration of its own, [v1k70rk4/HASS.Agent.NET10-Integration](https://github.com/v1k70rk4/HASS.Agent.NET10-Integration), in the Marketplace (HACS in Home Assistant before 2026.11). It creates every entity of a Windows PC from what the app advertises, over MQTT or the Home Assistant API, and removes what you switch off. The app and the integration are released with matching version numbers; a feature that needs a newer integration says so on its page.

These pages are the integration's own README, shown here so that the two sides are in one place; the [README on GitHub](https://github.com/v1k70rk4/HASS.Agent.NET10-Integration#readme) is the original, and its [changelog](https://github.com/v1k70rk4/HASS.Agent.NET10-Integration/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) lists its releases.
